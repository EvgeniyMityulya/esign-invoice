Who uses Inko

Invoices, quotes and releases for photographers

The shoot is booked on a phone, the release is signed on a phone, the invoice follows from the same document.

Photography runs on agreements made quickly and in person. A booking confirmed by message, a release signed on the day, an invoice after delivery. All three are the same document at different stages, and all three can happen on the device in your hand.

A shoot through the app

  1. Quote the shoot with the day rate, travel and licensing as separate lines
  2. The client signs the quote when booking, so the date is held
  3. On the day, a release or a model agreement is signed the same way
  4. After delivery, the quote becomes the invoice

Licensing belongs on the document

Personal use, commercial use, social only, print. Written as its own line with its own price, licensing stops being an awkward conversation six months later when the photo turns up on a billboard.

Signatures on location

A release signed on your phone at the shoot beats one emailed afterwards, because afterwards half of them never come back. It takes fifteen seconds and the record is kept with the document.

Travel, prints and extras

The price book keeps the lines you use on every job, so a quote is assembled rather than typed. Prints and albums carry their own units and currency where they need to.
