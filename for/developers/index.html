Who uses Inko

Invoicing for developers and IT contractors

Milestones, retainers, clients abroad.

What breaks in contract work?

The work is remote, the client is often abroad, and the money arrives in their currency. What holds it together is a scope agreed in writing rather than on a call.

A project through the app

  1. Write the estimate as milestones rather than hours, so scope is visible
  2. Get it signed before the first commit, in person or on your screen at a kickoff
  3. Invoice per milestone from the same document, no retyping
  4. Track paid and overdue per client rather than in a spreadsheet

Billing across borders

Ninety one currencies are built in, so a client paying in euros, pounds, dirhams or tenge gets an invoice in their own denomination rather than a converted guess. Your reports still group by currency, so nothing is silently mixed.

The estimate is your scope document

A signed estimate is the cheapest protection against scope creep that exists. It costs nothing, it takes a minute, and it turns "we assumed that was included" into a document with a date on it.

Your client list is not a spreadsheet

Clients, their documents and their totals live together, so answering "what has this client paid this year" takes one tap rather than a search through a folder of PDFs.
