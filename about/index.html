About Inko

Paperwork finished on your phone.
Estimates, invoices and signatures, without a laptop or an account.

Documents

Estimate, signature, invoice

Build the document in a minute, hand over the phone, and the client signs with a finger. The signed estimate becomes an invoice in one tap, so nothing gets retyped.

Payments

Paid directly, with no cut

A payment QR code sends the client to your own PayPal, Stripe or Revolut. Inko never holds the money and takes no commission.

Security

Nothing leaves the phone

No accounts and no server. Clients, documents and signatures stay on your iPhone, which also means the app works with no signal on site.
