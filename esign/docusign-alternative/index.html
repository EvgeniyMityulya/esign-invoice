Signing on a phone

A DocuSign alternative for people who sign a few documents

Paying monthly to sign twice a year.

Why does the price feel wrong?

The big signing services route documents between people in different buildings, and they price for that routing. If your customer is standing next to you, routing is the part you never use.

What is Inko built for?

The moment both people are in the same place. A quote agreed at a kitchen table, a release signed at a shoot, a contract signed across a desk. Everything in the app is shaped around that minute, which is why it needs no account, no signal and no second app for the other person.

Why does that make it faster?

Nothing leaves the device and nobody waits for an email, so the whole exchange happens while the customer is still in front of you. One tap turns the signed estimate into an invoice, and the record of who signed and when is saved next to it.

What do you get?

A signed PDF in under a minute, a completion certificate with the time and the parties, ninety one currencies, three premium templates, and your documents staying on your own phone. No commission on anything you get paid.
