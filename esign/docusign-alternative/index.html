Signing on a phone

A DocuSign alternative for people who sign a few documents

The free plan died in 2022.

Why does the price feel wrong?

DocuSign routes documents between people in different buildings, and prices for it. If your customer is standing next to you, that routing is the part you never use.

Two different jobs

Signing happensDocuSign: by email, laterInko: in front of you, now
The other person needsDocuSign: a link and often an accountInko: nothing, they use your phone
Works with no signalDocuSign: noInko: yes, everything is on the device
Your documents liveDocuSign: on their serversInko: on your phone
Cost to sign one documentDocuSign: a subscriptionInko: free tier, no watermark

When is DocuSign still the right answer?

Remote parties, several signers in a set order, corporate audit requirements, integration with a CRM. If that is the job, pay for the tool that does it. Nothing here pretends otherwise.

What do you give up going smaller?

No sending for remote signature, no reminder emails, no template library shared across a team. Inko is one person with a phone and a customer in front of them.

What do you get back?

A signed PDF in under a minute, a record of who signed and when, and no monthly fee for the two documents a year you actually sign.
